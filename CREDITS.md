# Credits

**Reborn in Elonth — Prolog** is entirely original. Every image in the film is
drawn with code (Canvas 2D) and every sound is synthesised procedurally with
Tone.js at render time.

## External audio files

None. No sampled, recorded or downloaded sounds or music are used (so there are
no CC0 files to list). Rain, heartbeat, horn, brake squeal, the lullaby,
music box, strings and piano are all generated by `src/audio/score.ts`.

## External images

None. The files in `reference/` are the project's own character sheets and
were only used as colour/design references; they are not shown in the film.

## Fonts (bundled via npm, SIL Open Font License 1.1)

- **Cinzel** — Natanael Gama (`@fontsource/cinzel`), used for the title card.
- **Noto Sans** — Google / Noto project (`@fontsource/noto-sans`), used for subtitles and UI.

## Libraries

- [GSAP](https://gsap.com/) — timeline / easing (GreenSock standard license)
- [Tone.js](https://tonejs.github.io/) — synthesis and offline audio rendering (MIT)
- [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/), [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/), [Playwright](https://playwright.dev/) (MIT / Apache-2.0)
- [ffmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org/) — muxing the exported MP4 (LGPL/GPL, used as an external tool)

## Invented in-film names

The RPG on Nolan's monitor ("AETHERFALL · Ember Chronicle"), its sprites and UI,
the phone and the status window are invented for this film. No real game,
brand or logo appears.
